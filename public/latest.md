### 1. Retailgentic: Behavior Beats Identity: FERMÀT Co-Founder & CEO Rishabh Jain
**Score:** 84/100 | **Listen Fully** | low confidence
**Duration:** 44 min

This episode delves into the evolving landscape of e-commerce personalization, specifically focusing on FERMÀT's AI-native platform. It highlights a shift from identity-based personalization to behavior-based personalization, arguing that this approach yields significantly better conversion rates, even for anonymous visitors. The discussion covers how AI agents can detect, recommend, and generate new site experiences in real-time, contrasting this with traditional, slower methods. The co-founder also shares insights on the limitations of the 'one-to-one' marketing dream and the strategic implications for incumbent personalization platforms, alongside candid advice on company building.

**Key Ideas:**
- FERMÀT's data indicates that behavior-based personalization, even for incognito visitors, can achieve approximately a 20% conversion lift compared to identity and past purchase-based methods.
- The 'one-to-one' marketer's dream is a 'lack of imagination' because a single individual acts as different shoppers across platforms like Instagram, Google, and ChatGPT, necessitating a 'many-to-one' personalization approach.
- AI agents trained on commerce can continuously monitor every session, recommend changes, and generate new site experiences, replacing the slow, human-intensive process of analyzing session recordings and implementing changes months later.
